# Student Academic Performance Analysis Report

## 1. Project Overview
This project evaluates academic performance data across primary and secondary student cohorts. By examining exam scores, homework consistency, and attendance records across multiple subjects (Math, Science, English, Arabic, History, and Geography), this study identifies key trends affecting student success and provides actionable recommendations.

## 2. Key Insights and Findings
* **Score Distribution:** Exam scores average approximately 75 marks across all subjects, indicating standard normalization across grading criteria.
* **Attendance Impact:** A direct positive trend exists between attendance rates and final exam results. Students maintaining attendance above 85% achieve significantly higher proportions of Grade A and Grade B classifications.
* **Subject-Level Comparison:** Performance across core disciplines (Math, Science, English, Social Sciences) remains evenly distributed with minimal variance in average scores.
* **Engagement Indicators:** Consistent homework completion directly reflects retention and correlates with lower failure rates in major examinations.

## 3. Recommendations
* **Early Intervention Programs:** Deploy automated alert notifications when a student's attendance drops below 75% to trigger counselor guidance.
* **Targeted Remedial Support:** Establish specialized study clinics for students scoring in the Grade D range (<60) prior to term examinations.
* **Parent-Teacher Engagement:** Expand structured communication logs to provide bi-weekly attendance and homework progress summaries to guardians.
